Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting paid advertising with Sigma.

How much ad spend budget do I need to start? +

We recommend starting with a minimum testing ad spend budget that allows Meta or TikTok algorithms to gather enough conversion data. During our strategy call, we calculate your exact minimum ad spend based on your product price and target customer acquisition cost (CAC).

Why do you direct traffic to WhatsApp instead of just a website? +

In Nigeria, trust and high-touch communication are paramount. While e-commerce websites work well for low-ticket visual items, directing ad leads into a frictionless WhatsApp conversation often yields significantly higher conversion rates, immediate customer relationship building, and higher bank-transfer/delivery success.

How quickly will my campaigns go live? +

Once onboarding is complete, campaign setup, audience research, technical tracking setup, and ad creative drafting typically takes 5 to 7 business days before official launch.

Do you guarantee sales or ROAS? +

No honest advertising agency promises guaranteed sales, because ad platforms operate on auction dynamics and sales depend on product demand, customer service speed, and fulfillment. However, we engineer controlled testing frameworks that shut down unprofitable ads quickly and scale winning angles systematically to protect your capital.

What if I have never run paid ads before? +

That is our specialty. We handle everything end-to-end: business manager setup, domain verification, tracking setup, creative scripting, targeting, and launch. You won't have to struggle through complex ad dashboards.
