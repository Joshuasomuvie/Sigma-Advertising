01 / THE DIAGNOSIS

The Nigerian Ad Trap: Why Most Campaigns Fail

Most Nigerian businesses aren't failing at advertising because their products are bad. They are failing because they mistake "boosting posts" for building a customer acquisition architecture.

The Typical "Boosted Post" Approach

  • Pressing the "Boost" button inside Instagram or Facebook with generic targeting.
  • Flooded with low-intent "How much?" comments that ghost after hearing the price.
  • No dedicated sales script or structured offer handling in DMs or WhatsApp.
  • Zero retargeting—paying repeatedly to reach new people while ignoring warm leads.
  • No clarity on Cost Per Customer Acquisition (CAC) or Return on Ad Spend (ROAS).

The Sigma Acquisition Engine

  • Structured Meta & TikTok Ads Manager setups using custom campaign objectives.
  • Direct-response copy and angle-tested creatives that pre-qualify buyers before they click.
  • Directing traffic straight to high-converting WhatsApp sales environments or landing pages.
  • Custom retargeting sequences that convert hesitant prospects over days, not seconds.
  • Strict focus on metrics that matter: Ad Spend → Qualified Leads → Closed Sales → Net Profit.

05 / CLARITY

Frequently Asked Questions

How is ad spend paid? +
Ad spend is separate from our management fee. You pay your ad budget directly to Meta, TikTok, or Google using your own business card or payment account inside your Ads Manager. We guide you on setting up local payment options safely.
Why do you focus so heavily on WhatsApp? +
In Nigeria, consumer trust is established in direct conversations. While traditional e-commerce works for some products, direct WhatsApp pathways allow Nigerian buyers to ask questions, verify stock, confirm delivery options, and build confidence before transferring funds.
How long does it take to see results after launching? +
Campaign setup, creative build, and funnel design take 3–5 business days. Once launched, campaigns begin driving initial clicks and leads within 24 to 48 hours. The first 7 to 14 days serve as our optimization window to refine target audience feedback and stabilize your acquisition costs.
Do you create the ad graphics and videos? +
Yes. We handle direct-response ad creative design, image assets, video scripts, and editing. Where physical product footage is required, we provide exact filming directions for your team or collaborate to gather raw product media.
