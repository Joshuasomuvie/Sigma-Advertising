Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting paid advertising with Sigma.

How much ad spend budget do I need to start? +

We recommend starting with a minimum testing ad spend budget that allows Meta or TikTok algorithms to gather enough conversion data. During our strategy call, we calculate your exact minimum ad spend based on your product price and target customer acquisition cost (CAC).

Why WhatsApp, a landing page, or something else for conversions? +

It depends on the business and the customer. For many Nigerian businesses, WhatsApp still converts leads more reliably than a cold website, but a landing page or booking flow can outperform it elsewhere. We choose the conversion path based on how your customers actually buy, not a default template.

How quickly will my campaigns go live? +

Once onboarding is complete, campaign setup, audience research, technical tracking setup, and ad creative drafting typically takes 5 to 7 business days before official launch.

Do you guarantee sales or ROAS? +

No honest advertising agency promises guaranteed sales, because ad platforms operate on auction dynamics and results depend on product demand, customer service speed, and fulfillment. What we control is the process: a testing framework that shuts down unprofitable ads quickly and scales winning angles systematically, to protect your budget.

What if I have never run paid ads before? +

That is our specialty. We handle everything end-to-end: business manager setup, domain verification, tracking setup, creative scripting, targeting, and launch. You won't have to struggle through complex ad dashboards.

How much does working with Sigma cost? +

Management fees depend on scope, the channels involved, campaign complexity, and how much ongoing management your business needs. We quote this transparently after an initial conversation about your business. Your ad spend is paid directly to Meta, TikTok, or Google and is always separate from our fee.
